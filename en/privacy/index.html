This policy explains how Seksen: YDS Soru Bankası ("Seksen") for iPhone and Android, and this website, handle personal data. It describes the iPhone and Android versions of the app and applies wherever you live. Where something applies only to one platform or only to the versions in which Premium is offered, we say so there. The Premium subscription is covered in section 4A, and the KVKK notice for users in Türkiye is in section 12.

In short

  • Seksen has no accounts; there is no sign-up or sign-in. Seksen has no server.
  • The questions you answer, your progress and your settings are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to any server.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools; how you use the app is not sent to anyone. We do not sell personal data.
  • Seksen Premium is an optional subscription. Only the store sells Premium (the App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android) and the store takes the payment. For Premium the app talks only to the store system on your phone; it sends no information to us or to any other company (section 4A).
  • As of 25 November 2026 the iPhone app on the App Store has no Premium and no in-app purchases.
  • If your phone's own backup is on (iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), the app's data may be included in your backup. We cannot see these backups (section 3).
  • We only receive your email if you write to us, and we use it only to reply to you.
  • Questions: seksenapp@gmail.com

3. Your phone's backups

Phones' own backup features may include app data (including Seksen's information above) in your backup. Seksen does not do this; Apple or Google does it according to your phone's settings, and their own privacy policies apply to these services.

iPhone

  • If iCloud Backup is on, or if you back up your iPhone to a computer, Seksen's data may be included in that backup. The backup stays in your Apple account or on your computer.
  • Neither Seksen nor we have any way to access this backup; it is not sent to us.
  • On a new iPhone restored from the backup, your progress may come back.
  • The iCloud Backup setting is in the iPhone Settings app, under your name, in the iCloud section (menu names may vary by iOS version).

Android

  • If Android's backup is on, Android may upload the app's data to a private backup area in your Google account. The backup is protected by your account credentials; on Android 9 and later, if your phone has a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password), the backup is end-to-end encrypted with it.
  • Neither Seksen nor we have any way to access this backup; it is not sent to us.
  • When you reinstall Seksen or move to a new phone, Android may restore your progress from this backup. So deleting the app does not immediately delete the backup in your Google account.
  • Only the latest backup is kept for each app; a new backup replaces the old one. If you reset your progress, the next backup replaces the old one with the reset state.
  • To turn backup off, see Settings > Google > All services > Backup on your phone (menu names may vary by phone). This setting affects your phone's whole Google backup, not only Seksen.

4. What Seksen does not collect or use

  • Account information: there are no accounts. Seksen does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos and files: Seksen asks for no permission to these.
  • Analytics, crash reporting, advertising and tracking tools: there are none in the app. How you use the app (which screens you open, how long you study) is not sent to anyone. Seksen does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. There are no ads in the app.
  • Payments: in the versions in which Premium is offered, the store takes the payment; your card and payment details never reach us (section 4A). In versions without Premium there are no in-app purchases.
  • Internet: questions, explanations, word cards and the exam calendar ship inside the app and are not downloaded. Seksen has no server of its own; the app does not connect to us or to any other company's server. In the versions in which Premium is offered, the app uses only the store system on your phone for Premium; the store itself talks to its own servers when needed (section 4A). Your study data is not sent through this. Building blocks used in Android apps add the internet permission automatically; Seksen sends no data with it. When you tap the privacy link in the app, this page opens in your browser.
  • The pictures on word cards are AI-generated illustrations made for this app. They ship inside the app, are not downloaded and use no personal data.

4A. Premium and subscriptions

This section applies only to versions of the app in which Premium is offered. As of 25 November 2026 the iPhone app on the App Store has no Premium; when Premium comes to iPhone with an app update, this section applies to iPhone in the same way.

You buy Seksen Premium inside the app, through the store's own payment sheet: the App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android. The store takes the payment. We have no server for subscriptions and use no other subscription service provider.

  • What the app does: for Premium it talks only to the store system on your phone (the App Store on iPhone, Google Play Billing on Android). It gets the plans, the prices and whether you are eligible for a free trial from the store; it passes your purchase and restore requests to the store; and when the app opens or comes back to the foreground it asks the store whether your subscription is still active. This check happens even if you do not buy Premium; the store handles the check and its answer, and we do not see them.
  • What the store processes: your store account, your payment method, your purchase and subscription history, and your trial and renewal status. Apple and Google process these under their own privacy policies and on their own responsibility.
  • What stays on your phone: the subscription summary the store last confirmed (table in section 2). It is kept so that Premium also works offline, and it is not sent to us. The purchase information the store gives the app during a purchase (for example the Google Play purchase token) is used only at that moment to acknowledge the purchase with the store and to verify the subscription; it is not stored in the app and not sent to us or anywhere else.
  • Not sent: your name, email address, phone number, location, advertising identifier, answers, progress and settings. For Premium the app sends no information to us or to any company other than the store.
  • What we see: the stores give sellers sales reports. Google Play shows a sales order record for each sale in its seller console (for example the order number, product, date, amount, country or region and order status). Apple gives only aggregate sales reports; no record identifying an App Store buyer reaches us. We use these records only for our accounting and tax obligations, refunds and disputes, and to solve subscription problems you write to us about.
  • Purpose: to sell, turn on, verify and restore Premium on a new phone; to perform the subscription contract; to meet our legal obligations. This information is not used to track you or for advertising.
  • Retention: order records stay in the store's system under the store's rules. Reports we download for accounting are kept for the period required by tax law.
  • Deletion: you delete the subscription summary on your phone by deleting the app. Store records are Apple's or Google's responsibility; contact the store about them. If you have a request about an order record we can see, write to seksenapp@gmail.com with your order number (on Google Play it starts with GPA.); we answer within 30 days at the latest for the part not covered by our legal retention obligation. Such a request does not cancel your subscription; you cancel in the store.
  • Security: the store runs the communication between the app and the store with its own security measures. Only we can access the seller console.

5. Notifications, pronunciation and other phone features

Exam reminders

If you turn on a reminder on the exam calendar screen, Seksen asks for notification permission; it does not ask at first launch. Reminders are set on the phone with its own local notifications. There is no server and no remote (push) notification. If you do not allow notifications, no reminder is set and the rest of the app keeps working. On Android there are also standard system permissions to set scheduled reminders again after the phone restarts and for vibration; they read no information from you.

Pronunciation (Listen)

Pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Seksen passes to it only the English sentence or word from the app; nothing you type is read. If an English voice is installed on your phone, no internet is needed. The text-to-speech engine is software from your phone or its manufacturer and follows its own rules.

Widget (iPhone only)

If you add the widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, it shows the days left to your exam and your streak, from the information in the table in section 2. This information does not leave the phone.

Feedback and bug reports

The "Send feedback" row ("Geri bildirim gönder") in Settings opens your phone's email app with a new draft addressed to seksenapp@gmail.com. Seksen itself sends nothing; you write the email and, if you want, you send it. Only Seksen's version number and your phone's operating system version are written at the bottom of the draft.

On Android, Settings also has a "Report a bug" row ("Hata bildir"). It works the same way; to help us understand the problem, the bottom of the draft contains Seksen's version and build number, the Android version and your phone's manufacturer and model name (for example "Samsung SM-A515F"). This is read from the phone's system information; no permission is asked for it.

Neither draft contains a device identifier, advertising identifier or your progress. You see the whole draft before sending and can delete anything you like.

6. If you email us

Writing to us is optional and not needed to use the app.

  • Retention: we delete your email 12 months after your request is resolved. You can ask us to delete it earlier.

The app's information stays in the app area that only Seksen can access, protected by your phone's own security (for example the screen lock). Only we and our email service provider can access the emails you send us. Only we can access the stores' seller consoles. No system is completely secure; please do not send sensitive information by email. If a security incident affects the information we hold, we will notify the relevant authority and you within the time the law requires.

10. Children

Seksen is designed for adults preparing for the YDS and e-YDS exams; it is not directed at children. On Google Play, the app's target audience is set to 18 and over. Seksen does not knowingly collect personal data from children. A Premium purchase can be made by people the store account allows and under the store's rules (for example parental approval). If you are under 18, talk to your parent or guardian before writing to us. If we learn that a child has written to us, we delete the message; you can tell us about it.

11. Your rights

The information we may hold about you is the email you sent us and, if you bought Premium on Google Play, the order record Google Play shows us (section 4A). We need your order number to find that record. Wherever you live, you can ask us about this information: whether we hold information about you and a copy of it, to correct or delete it, and to object to its processing. If you have never written to us and never bought Premium on Google Play, we hold no information that identifies you.

  • Requests: seksenapp@gmail.com. To verify your identity we use only the information we already have (the email address you wrote from, or the order number).
  • We reply in writing, free of charge, within 30 days at the latest; if the law where you live sets a shorter period, that period applies.
  • We do not base any processing on your consent, so there is no consent to withdraw. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.
  • You can delete the app data on your phone yourself: Settings > Reset progress, or delete the app (for backups see section 3).
  • For purchase and subscription information in your store account, you can contact Apple or Google.
  • If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live (for Türkiye, see section 12).

Users outside Türkiye

Seksen is offered on the App Store and Google Play in all countries except mainland China. Wherever you are, the app works the same way: your app data stays on your phone; the information that can reach us is the email you send us and, if you bought Premium on Google Play, the order record in section 4A. If the law where you live gives you broader rights, those rights apply too, and you can use them all through the same address.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: we process your emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering your questions (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and you can object to this processing (Art. 21). We process order records to perform the subscription contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and to meet our accounting and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15-21). Processing of emails abroad is described in section 6; store records are kept in Apple's and Google's systems under their rules. You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work: EEA list at edpb.europa.eu, in the UK the ICO, in Switzerland the FDPIC.
  • United States (including California): when you write to us we receive identifiers (your email address) and customer records (the content of your message); if you bought Premium on Google Play, Google Play shows us commercial information (the order record). The purpose and retention period are in sections 4A and 6. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not process sensitive personal information. You have the rights to know and access, delete and correct, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. An authorized agent may submit a request for you with your written permission; we may verify your identity with you directly. The 30-day reply period above applies here too.
