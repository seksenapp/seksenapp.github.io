Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective and last updated: 14 November 2026

Türkçe sürüm (the Turkish text prevails, see section 14)

This policy explains how Seksen: YDS Soru Bankası ("Seksen") for iPhone and Android, and this website, handle personal data. It describes the current versions of the app (iPhone 1.0, Android 1.0.0) and applies wherever you live. The KVKK notice for users in Türkiye is in section 12.

In short

  • Seksen has no accounts; there is no sign-up or sign-in.
  • The questions you answer, your progress and your settings are stored only on your phone. The app does not send them to us or to any server.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell personal data.
  • If your phone's own backup is on (iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), the app's data may be included in your backup. We cannot see these backups (section 3).
  • We only receive your email if you write to us, and we use it only to reply to you.
  • Questions: seksenapp@gmail.com

3. Your phone's backups

Phones' own backup features may include app data (including Seksen's information above) in your backup. Seksen does not do this; Apple or Google does it according to your phone's settings, and their own privacy policies apply to these services.

iPhone

  • If iCloud Backup is on, or if you back up your iPhone to a computer, Seksen's data may be included in that backup. The backup stays in your Apple account or on your computer.
  • Neither Seksen nor we have any way to access this backup; it is not sent to us.
  • On a new iPhone restored from the backup, your progress may come back.
  • The iCloud Backup setting is in the iPhone Settings app, under your name, in the iCloud section (menu names may vary by iOS version).

Android

  • If Android's backup is on, Android may upload the app's data to a private backup area in your Google account. The backup is protected by your account credentials; on Android 9 and later, if your phone has a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password), the backup is end-to-end encrypted with it.
  • Neither Seksen nor we have any way to access this backup; it is not sent to us.
  • When you reinstall Seksen or move to a new phone, Android may restore your progress from this backup. So deleting the app does not immediately delete the backup in your Google account.
  • Only the latest backup is kept for each app; a new backup replaces the old one. If you reset your progress, the next backup replaces the old one with the reset state.
  • To turn backup off, see Settings > Google > All services > Backup on your phone (menu names may vary by phone). This setting affects your phone's whole Google backup, not only Seksen.

4. What Seksen does not collect or use

  • Account information: there are no accounts. Seksen does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos and files: Seksen asks for no permission to these.
  • Analytics, crash reporting, advertising and tracking tools: there are none in the app. Seksen does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Payments: these versions have no ads and no in-app purchases; no payment data is processed.
  • Internet: the app's own code does not connect to the internet; questions, explanations, word cards and the exam calendar ship inside the app. Building blocks used in Android apps add the internet permission automatically; Seksen sends no data with it. When you tap the privacy link in the app, this page opens in your browser.
  • The pictures on word cards are AI-generated illustrations made for this app. They ship inside the app, are not downloaded and use no personal data.

5. Notifications, pronunciation and other phone features

Exam reminders

If you turn on a reminder on the exam calendar screen, Seksen asks for notification permission; it does not ask at first launch. Reminders are set on the phone with its own local notifications. There is no server and no remote (push) notification. If you do not allow notifications, no reminder is set and the rest of the app keeps working. On Android there are also standard system permissions to set scheduled reminders again after the phone restarts and for vibration; they read no information from you.

Pronunciation (Listen)

Pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Seksen passes to it only the English sentence or word from the app; nothing you type is read. If an English voice is installed on your phone, no internet is needed. The text-to-speech engine is software from your phone or its manufacturer and follows its own rules.

Widget (iPhone only)

If you add the widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, it shows the days left to your exam and your streak, from the information in the table in section 2. This information does not leave the phone.

Feedback and bug reports

The "Send feedback" row ("Geri bildirim gönder") in Settings opens your phone's email app with a new draft addressed to seksenapp@gmail.com. Seksen itself sends nothing; you write the email and, if you want, you send it. Only Seksen's version number and your phone's operating system version are written at the bottom of the draft.

On Android, Settings also has a "Report a bug" row ("Hata bildir"). It works the same way; to help us understand the problem, the bottom of the draft contains Seksen's version and build number, the Android version and your phone's manufacturer and model name (for example "Samsung SM-A515F"). This is read from the phone's system information; no permission is asked for it.

Neither draft contains a device identifier, advertising identifier or your progress. You see the whole draft before sending and can delete anything you like.

6. If you email us

Writing to us is optional and not needed to use the app.

  • What we receive: your email address, your name if you include it, your message and anything you attach (for example a screenshot, the technical details in section 5).
  • Purpose: to reply to you and fix the problems you report (for example a wrong answer key). We do not use it for advertising or marketing and do not sell it.
  • Who processes it: our email is received through Google's email service (Gmail). Google may process these emails in other countries, including the United States, where protection may differ from where you live. This transfer happens only when you write to us and only as far as needed to reply.
  • Retention: we delete your email 12 months after your request is resolved. You can ask us to delete it earlier.

7. Sharing and selling

The app shares no data with any third party, and your app data does not reach us. Emails you send us are kept only in the systems of our email service provider (Google) and may be shared with competent authorities only when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for targeted advertising as defined in US state laws. If data ever needs to be shared with a third party, we will first update this policy and require that party to give your data protection equal to the protection in this policy.

8. App Store, Google Play and this page

  • You download Seksen from the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle downloads and updates under their own privacy policies. The stores may show us statistics such as total downloads, and crash and performance information from users who chose in their phone settings to share analytics or diagnostics with developers. The stores collect this information; we do not use it to identify anyone.
  • This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or tracking code. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes; this log stays with GitHub, and we keep no visitor records.

9. Security

The app's information stays in the app area that only Seksen can access, protected by your phone's own security (for example the screen lock). Only we and our email service provider can access the emails you send us. No system is completely secure; please do not send sensitive information by email. If a security incident affects emails you sent us, we will notify the relevant authority and you within the time the law requires.

10. Children

Seksen is designed for adults preparing for the YDS and e-YDS exams; it is not directed at children. On Google Play, the app's target audience is set to 18 and over. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If you are under 18, talk to your parent or guardian before writing to us. If we learn that a child has written to us, we delete the message; you can tell us about it.

11. Your rights

The only information we may hold about you is the email you sent us. Wherever you live, you can ask us about these emails: whether we hold information about you and a copy of it, to correct or delete it, and to object to its processing. If you have never written to us, we hold no information about you.

  • Requests: seksenapp@gmail.com. To verify your identity we use only the information we already have (the email address you wrote from).
  • We reply in writing, free of charge, within 30 days at the latest; if the law where you live sets a shorter period, that period applies.
  • We do not base any processing on your consent, so there is no consent to withdraw. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.
  • You can delete the app data on your phone yourself: Settings > Reset progress, or delete the app (for backups see section 3).
  • If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live (for Türkiye, see section 12).

Users outside Türkiye

Seksen is offered on the App Store and Google Play in all countries except mainland China. Wherever you are, the app works the same way: your app data stays on your phone, and the only information that can reach us is the email you send us. If the law where you live gives you broader rights, those rights apply too, and you can use them all through the same address.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: we process your emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering your questions (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and you can object to this processing (Art. 21). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15-21). Transfers abroad are described in section 6. You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work: EEA list at edpb.europa.eu, in the UK the ICO, in Switzerland the FDPIC.
  • United States (including California): only when you write to us do we receive identifiers (your email address) and customer records (the content of your message); the purpose and retention period are in section 6. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not process sensitive personal information. You have the rights to know and access, delete and correct, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. An authorized agent may submit a request for you with your written permission; we may verify your identity with you directly. The 30-day reply period above applies here too.
